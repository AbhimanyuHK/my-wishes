# Google Play listing draft — My Wishes

**App name:** My Wishes

**Short description (draft):** Create and share personalized greeting cards, even offline.

**Full description:**

Celebrate meaningful moments with My Wishes, an offline greeting-card creator for Android.

Browse greetings in English and Kannada, choose an occasion, and personalize a card with your own message, name, and photo. Preview your creation, save it as an image, or share it using your favorite messaging app.

Features:
- Browse greeting categories and select ready-to-use wishes.
- Discover upcoming supported fixed-date observances.
- Create custom wishes and personalized greeting cards.
- Add a profile photo and signature to your designs.
- Save, revisit, and share generated cards.
- Switch between English and Kannada.
- Create cards without an internet connection.

**Accuracy note:** Date-based suggestions currently cover a selected set of fixed-date observances for 2026–2030; not all regional or movable festivals are included.
