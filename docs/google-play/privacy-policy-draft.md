## Information processed on your device
Depending on the features you use, My Wishes may process a display name, signature, selected profile image, chosen greetings, favorite greetings, preferences, and generated card images. The application is designed to keep this information in app-private local storage or device-selected export locations.

## Sharing and exporting
When you choose to share a generated card, Android opens a system share flow and sends the selected image to the destination you choose. Any receiving app or service processes that image under its own privacy terms. Exported images are stored in the location you choose and may remain after uninstalling My Wishes.
